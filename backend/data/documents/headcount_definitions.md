# Headcount and Workforce Metrics Definitions

Workforce reporting adheres to standardized analytical criteria:
- **Total Workforce**: Represents all 590 historic records cataloged in the ETS operational dataset.
- **Active Headcount**: Refers strictly to the 557 employees whose exit date is null and whose current status is recorded as Active.
- **Exited Employees**: Represents the 33 individuals who have resigned, retired, or completed assignments between 2018 and 2024.
- **Gender Distribution**: Across the active organization baseline, recorded demographics comprise 408 males (69.15%) and 182 females (30.85%).
