# Compensation Structure and Promotion Guidelines

Total Cost to Company (CTC) is structured across four primary components:
1. **Base Salary**: Constitutes approximately 80% to 85% of total annual compensation.
2. **Performance Bonus**: Variable component representing roughly 10% of annual compensation tied to business and individual appraisal scores.
3. **Perks and Allowances**: Standard benefits representing approximately 3% of compensation.
4. **Other Compensation**: Supplementary components representing roughly 2% of annual compensation.

## Standard Salary Bins
Workforce earnings are benchmarked within six standardized compensation bands:
- `<5L`: Under 5 Lakhs INR
- `5-10L`: 5 to 10 Lakhs INR
- `10-15L`: 10 to 15 Lakhs INR
- `15-20L`: 15 to 20 Lakhs INR
- `20-30L`: 20 to 30 Lakhs INR
- `>30L`: Above 30 Lakhs INR

## Promotion Hike Criteria
Promotion events require elevation to the next grade level (e.g. E3 to E4) accompanied by a standardized compensation hike. Historical compensation is tracked annually from 2020 through 2024 with promotion events, hike percentages, and grade adjustments.
