# Grade Progression Ladder Policy

The organization follows a strict 10-tier grade hierarchy: E1, E2, E3, E4, E5, E6, E7, E8, E9, E10.

## Grade Bands and Job Titles

- **E1–E3**: Associate Engineers and Software Engineers. Entry-level to early career professionals focusing on core technical implementation.
- **E4–E5**: Senior Software Engineers and Technical Leads. Experienced engineers providing technical guidance, architectural review, and module leadership.
- **E6–E7**: Senior Technical Leads, Project Managers, and Lead Business Analysts. Leadership tier responsible for delivery accountability, project governance, and cross-functional orchestration.
- **E8–E10**: Senior Project Managers, Technical Managers, Program Managers, and Vice Presidents. Executive and strategic leadership managing multi-project portfolios, delivery practices, and enterprise capabilities.

Advancement between grades requires formal performance evaluations, competency assessment against role matrix, and tenure milestone eligibility.
