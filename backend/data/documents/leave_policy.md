# Leave Types and Attendance Tracking Rules

Leaves are recorded daily in the attendance system with specific categorization codes:
- **CL (Casual Leave)**: Discretionary short-term time off intended for personal exigencies.
- **SL (Sick Leave)**: Medical and health recovery leave supported by medical documentation when exceeding two consecutive working days.
- **EL (Earned Leave)**: Accrued annual vacation leave earned periodically across continuous active tenure.
- **CO (Compensatory Off)**: Time-off credit granted when employees work on designated rest days, weekends, or during critical project delivery crunch periods.

## Day Value Accounting
All leave requests are accounted as:
- `0.5`: Half-day session (morning or afternoon).
- `1.0`: Full-day absence.
Absences are aggregated by department, project, and location to compute delivery staffing availability.
