# Executive Compensation and Retention Incentive Plan

RESTRICTED DOCUMENT: Available only to executive leadership.

Senior executives at grade levels E8 through E10 participate in the Long-Term Executive Retention Plan (LTERP).
- Executive bonus pools are calibrated on EBITDA targets and client margin expansion.
- Discretionary retention awards cliff-vest over 36 months of continuous active tenure.
- Non-compete and client non-solicitation covenants apply for 12 months following exit.
