# Experience and Tenure Measurement Architecture

Tenure is evaluated through three canonical metrics within the analytics database:
1. **Infinite Experience**: Internal company tenure measured in years from Start Date to present or Exit Date. The overall organization average tenure is 2.89 years.
2. **Prior Experience**: Verified industry and professional experience prior to joining the company. The organization average prior experience is 3.21 years.
3. **Total Experience**: Cumulative sum of Prior Experience and Infinite Experience. The overall organization average total experience is 6.10 years.
